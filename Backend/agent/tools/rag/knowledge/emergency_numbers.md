# Emergency and Mental Health Hotlines by Country

This document contains verified emergency numbers, mental health hotlines, and suicide prevention resources for various countries.

## Saudi Arabia (SA)
- **National Center for Mental Health Promotion**: 9200 33360
- **Ministry of Health (Mental Health)**: 937
- **Medical Emergency (Ambulance)**: 997
- **Police Emergency**: 999

## United Arab Emirates (AE)
- **Ministry of Health (National Mental Support)**: 800 4673
- **Dubai Health Authority (Mental Health Helpline)**: 800 111
- **Abu Dhabi Mental Health Hotline**: 800 725462
- **Medical Emergency (Ambulance)**: 998

## Egypt (EG)

## Jordan (JO)

## Bahrain (BH)
- **Family Counseling Center**: 8000 1488
- **Shamsaha MENA**: 3844 7588 or 6671 0901

## Global Fallback (US, UK, CA, AU)
- **United States (US)**: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- **United Kingdom (GB)**: 111 (NHS non-emergency) or 999 (Emergency)
- **Canada (CA)**: 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline)
- **Australia (AU)**: 13 11 14 (Lifeline)

For any other country not listed above, please advise the user to contact their local emergency services, which is typically 112 or 911.
